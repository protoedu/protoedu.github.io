Intro

I'm Eduardo Sciammarella, the designer behind NanoFraction's holographic measurement platform. I help labs, diagnostic teams, and creative studios turn raw optical data into precision metrology, visualization data, and playable stories with community-building. I build real-world prototypes, whether you need high-grade lab instrumentation or visual design strategy, I bring the measurement-first toolkit to your toughest design problems.

I’m a graduate of the Institute of Design at Illinois Tech (founded as The New Bauhaus), and I’ve received multiple design and innovation honors. In Japan, I was a recipient of the Asahi Shimbun Company Award (FY2018 National Invention Awards, Japan Institute of Invention and Innovation, JIII) for inventing a touch-operated wireless device connection method, later standardized in the NFC Forum Connection Handover specification "Tap to Pay".
